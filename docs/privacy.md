# Privacy notice (draft)

**DRAFT. Every legal point here needs review by counsel before publication. Some facts are marked (unverified) or (proposed) and need owner decisions.** This is not legal advice. Reviewers: the owner and the back-end architect. The table-level source for this notice is `docs/data-inventory.md`. The statements about what is stored were checked against the code on 2026-11-03.

The Leeroy Living Board is an unofficial companion for guilds playing WoW Forever. It is a fan project, not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment. It runs on Cloudflare. This notice says what we hold about you, why, who else sees it, and what you can do.

## What we collect

| Category | What | Where it comes from |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Discord id | Your numeric Discord user id, in plain text in the account tables (`users`) and in the guild roster the owner keeps | Discord login (when web login is on) or a Discord interaction |
| Display name | Your Discord name, and the handle or nickname used on the board and in the roster | Discord, and the guild admin |
| Guild and role | Which Discord server's board you are on and your role there (owner, admin, member), where the owner ran `/setup` or you confirmed `/join` or `/build` | Discord, guild admin |
| Builds | Characters (name, realm, class, race and similar), talents, gear and character-sheet fields you or your guild enter, including a history of sheet edits | You |
| Sign-ups | Raid and event attendance you set | You |
| Saved settings | Preferences and journey state, saved when you sign in | You |
| Questions to Leeroy | What you type into Ask or a Discord command, the answer, which AI model answered, and a log of the request | You |
| Answer buttons | If switched on: the extra answer text behind "More" and "Sources", and a one-way hash of your Discord id with each Helpful / Off vote | You |
| Votes | Your choice in a board vote, kept under a one-way hash of your Discord id and the vote id | You |
| Usage counters | A daily count of AI use per person, kept under a short one-way hash of your Discord id (or of your board handle) that is not salted | Generated by the service |
| Guest visits | If you ask as a guest without a key: a salted one-way hash of your IP address and the time, to limit guests to a few questions an hour. The address itself is not stored by the service | Your device |

We do not store your Discord avatar, your email address, or any password. Nothing in the current code stores a Google, Battle.net, Twitch or GitHub identifier or an OAuth token; the database has tables ready for them (`identities`, `provider_tokens`) but no feature writes to them, and this notice will change before one does.

Cloudflare, which hosts the service, sees and logs request details such as your IP address, request time and browser type under its own terms (see Retention). The service does not keep a copy of them.

Parts of the board are public. The documents behind the wall (`party`, `signups`, `timeline`, `tonight` and a few more) are served to anyone who asks the API, with no sign-in: if your handle, character or sign-up appears there, anyone can read it.

## Message content disclosure

The bot answers slash commands and other interactions sent to it over HTTP (`POST /discord`). It does not listen to your channel messages (unverified; counsel and the owner should confirm no Message Content intent is enabled in the Discord developer portal). What you type into a command or into the Ask box is stored and sent to an AI vendor, so do not put anything private in it.

Two message actions are the exception. If you use the "Ask Leeroy" message action on someone's message, the text of that message (up to the question length limit) is stored as the question, in the Ask log and the intent log, and sent to the AI vendor. If you use "Fix this fact", the first 200 characters of that message are stored with a note and your name. In both cases the message may be written by someone else. Do not use them on messages that hold private information.

## Who receives it (recipients)

| Recipient | Why | What |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cloudflare | Hosting, database, Workers, Durable Objects, logs | Everything stored in the service |
| Discord | Login and bot | Your Discord id and the interactions you make |
| Blizzard | Nothing is sent to Blizzard. We use game names and data under their terms; we are not affiliated | None |
| AI vendor | Answering Ask | Your question and context. The vendor is whichever provider is configured (`worker/ai-models.js` lists several, among them Anthropic, Google, Groq, Cerebras, Cloudflare Workers AI and OpenRouter); see `docs/subprocessors.md` |
| Your guild admins | Running your guild's board | Your name, role, builds and sign-ups in that guild |

Anyone can read the public board documents described above, and other members can see what the board shows about you. Ask answers marked shared are visible to the group (`ask_turns.share` defaults to shared; a Discord command can mark a question private). If you click Helpful or Off on an answer, we keep a salted one-way hash of your Discord id with that vote so you can have one vote per answer; admins see counts and the question and answer, not who voted.

**We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.** Counsel to confirm wording for US state laws that define "sale" and "sharing" broadly.

## Retention

What the code does today, row by row. "Not deleted automatically" means nothing in the code removes the row; it stays until you use erase (below) or the owner deletes it.

| Data | Retention in code |
| :--- | :--- |
| Account (`users`), builds, characters, sign-ups, memberships | Not deleted automatically. Removed by the erase route when it is switched on |
| Web sessions | 30 days, at most 10 per person. An expired session is removed when it is next presented or when the same kind of sign-in happens again, not on a timer |
| Saved settings (`user_state`) and their sessions | Not deleted automatically (sessions as above). `DELETE /me` removes them |
| Questions and answers (Ask), sheet edits, intent log, fact fixes | Not deleted automatically. The intent log is cleared when the owner's bot acknowledges it |
| Guest IP hash (`guest_rate_limits`) | Rows older than 2 hours are deleted the next time any guest asks, so a row can outlast 2 hours when no guest asks |
| Extra answer text behind "More" and "Sources" (`answer_details`) | 30 days, deleted by the scheduled job (only when the answer buttons are switched on) |
| Helpful / Off votes (`answer_feedback`) | 90 days, deleted by the scheduled job (only when the answer buttons are switched on) |
| Votes and daily AI counters (`kv_state`) | Not deleted automatically |
| Audit log (`audit_log`) | Not deleted automatically (12 months is proposed) |
| Cloudflare request logs and AI Gateway logs and cached answers | Set by Cloudflare, not by this service (unverified; counsel to note). AI Gateway may cache an answer for up to 6 hours |
| Backups (D1 Time Travel) | Cloudflare's window (unverified; see `docs/runbooks/backup-restore.md`); deleted data can persist in backups until they expire |
| Owner backup files | The owner can pull a full export (`tools/pull-export.mjs`) and keeps it outside Cloudflare until deleted; it holds everything in the database except login secrets |
| Guild data after the guild removes the bot | Not deleted automatically (30 days then purge is proposed) |

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (counsel to confirm which laws apply and the response time): access, correction, deletion, export, objection or restriction, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to your regulator.

**What works today.** `DELETE /me` (with a web session) removes your saved settings and sessions and nothing else. The full export and erase (`GET /api/me/export`, `POST /api/me/erase`) are built and tested but are **switched off** (`DATA_RIGHTS` is off) until counsel and the owner turn them on; until then they answer 404. Until they are on, ask the owner (see Contact) to run an export or an erase for you.

**What the full erase removes**, once on: your account row and everything that hangs off it (sign-in identities, web sessions, memberships, characters, builds, sign-ups); your saved settings and sessions; your Helpful / Off votes; your ballot in each board vote; your daily AI counters; and, when you use your member key, the rows filed under your handle or name (sheet edits, dispatches, the intent log, your Ask questions and answers with the "More" / "Sources" text, and fact fixes). It leaves one audit row that says an erase happened and when, with no link to you.

**What it does not remove:** your card and name in the shared board documents (`party`, `signups`) and the owner's Discord roster, which an admin edits by hand; copies at Cloudflare (logs, AI Gateway cache), Discord and the AI vendor; and backups until they expire. A web-session erase cannot reach rows filed under a handle or name, because a Discord display name is chosen by its owner and could equal someone else's; use your member key for those. Guest questions are tied to a hashed IP address, not to you, and cannot be found by your id.

## Children

The service is not for anyone under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live if higher. Discord requires users to be 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if we learn we have, we delete it. (Counsel to confirm the threshold and any parental-consent duty.)

## International transfers, security, changes

Cloudflare and the AI vendors may process data outside your country; counsel to confirm the transfer mechanism. Member keys are stored only as salted hashes. Session tokens are stored only as one-way hashes of random 256-bit values (not salted, which is safe for random tokens). No system is perfectly secure. We will update this notice with a date when it changes.
