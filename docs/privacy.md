# Privacy notice (draft)

**DRAFT. Every legal point here needs review by counsel before publication. Some facts are marked (unverified) or (proposed) and need owner decisions.** This is not legal advice. Reviewers: the owner and the back-end architect. The table-level source for this notice is `docs/data-inventory.md`. The statements about what is stored were checked against the code on 2026-11-03.

The Leeroy Living Board is an unofficial companion for guilds playing WoW Forever. It is a fan project, not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment. It runs on Cloudflare. This notice says what we hold about you, why, who else sees it, and what you can do.

## What we collect

| Category | What | Where it comes from |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Discord id | Your numeric Discord user id, in plain text in the account tables (`users`) and in the guild roster the owner keeps | Discord login (when web login is on) or a Discord interaction |
| Display name | Your Discord name, and the handle or nickname used on the board and in the roster | Discord, and the guild admin |
| Guild and role | Which Discord server's board you are on and your role there (owner, admin, member), where the owner ran `/setup` or you confirmed `/join` or `/build` | Discord, guild admin |
| Builds | Characters (name, realm, class, race and similar), talents, gear and character-sheet fields you or your guild enter, including a history of sheet edits | You |
| Sign-ups | Raid and event attendance you set | You |
| Saved settings | Preferences and journey state, saved when you sign in | You |
| Questions to Leeroy | What you type into Ask or a Discord command, the answer, which AI model answered, and a log of the request. Who asked is kept as follows: a roster member under their roster name; a Discord user who is not on the roster under their Discord nickname; a visitor on the web as "Visitor", with nothing that identifies them. Since the 2026-11-03 change each new question also gets a salted one-way hash of the asker's Discord id (or, for a board key, of the member handle), used to find your questions for export and erase | You |
| Answer buttons | If switched on: the extra answer text behind "More" and "Sources", and a one-way hash of your Discord id with each Helpful / Off vote | You |
| Votes | Your choice in a board vote, kept under a one-way hash of your Discord id and the vote id | You |
| Usage counters | A daily count of AI use per person, kept under a short one-way hash of your Discord id (or of your board handle) that is not salted | Generated by the service |
| Guest visits | If you ask on the web as a guest without a key: a salted one-way hash of your IP address and the time, to limit guests to a few questions an hour. The address itself is not stored by the service. This row is not linked to your question: the question is stored as "Visitor" | Your device |

We do not store your Discord avatar, your email address, or any password. Nothing in the current code stores a Google, Battle.net, Twitch or GitHub identifier or an OAuth token; the database has tables ready for them (`identities`, `provider_tokens`) but no feature writes to them, and this notice will change before one does.

Cloudflare, which hosts the service, sees and logs request details such as your IP address, request time and browser type under its own terms (see Retention). The service does not keep a copy of them.

Parts of the board are public. The documents behind the wall (`party`, `signups`, `timeline`, `tonight` and a few more) are served to anyone who asks the API, with no sign-in: if your handle, character or sign-up appears there, anyone can read it.

## Your member key and what your browser keeps

- Your member key is stored **raw** (not hashed) in your browser's localStorage under the name `board-key`. The server keeps only a salted hash of it, but your browser keeps the key itself, so anyone who can open your browser profile, or any script that runs on this site, can read it.
- The key reaches the board in a link ending in `#k=<key>`. The page saves it and removes it from the address bar. The part after `#` is not sent to the server, but it can sit in your browser history and in anything you copy or share before it is removed. Treat a link with `#k=` like a password.
- The key is sent in an `Authorization` header only to the board's own service (`api.leeroy.party`, a preview copy of it, or a service on your own computer). It is not sent anywhere else.
- The board also keeps other settings in localStorage under names that start `board-` (such as your name, where you were and what you have seen) and short-lived copies of its data files under names that start `bd:`. "Forget me on this browser" removes every name that starts `board-` or `bd:`, including `board-key`.

## The device check (`/probe`)

The device check page reads what your browser reports and shows it to you first ("Nothing has been sent"). Only if you press Send does the board send it, to `POST /probe`, together with your member key in the `Authorization` header, so the report can be tied to your member. The report is 39 fields: a type tag (`kind`) and a version number (`v`), plus 37 readings:

- Browser and platform: engine, platform, whether it runs inside Discord, whether it has a Discord user agent, whether it is framed, whether it is installed as an app.
- Vibration and haptics: whether the vibration API exists and ran, whether the switch haptic works, the vibration route used, and the strength you said you felt.
- Motion sensors: whether the orientation and motion APIs exist, whether motion needs permission, the permission result, and how many orientation and motion events arrived in 3 seconds.
- Input: number of touch points, coarse pointer, no-hover, pointer events.
- Screen: screen width and height, window width and height, pixel ratio (times 100), visual viewport support, and the four safe-area insets (top, right, bottom, left).
- Preferences and display: reduced motion, data saver, HDR screen, wide-gamut screen, battery API, screen wake lock.

Each value is a yes/no or a small number. No name, no Discord id and no free text is in the report. At the time of writing the service acknowledges the report and does not store it (unverified: Cloudflare request logs may still show that the request was made).

## Message content disclosure

The bot answers slash commands and other interactions sent to it over HTTP (`POST /discord`). It does not listen to your channel messages (unverified; counsel and the owner should confirm no Message Content intent is enabled in the Discord developer portal). What you type into a command or into the Ask box is stored and sent to an AI vendor, so do not put anything private in it.

Two message actions are the exception. If you use the "Ask Leeroy" message action on someone's message, the text of that message (up to the question length limit) is stored as the question, in the Ask log and the intent log, and sent to the AI vendor. If you use "Fix this fact", the first 200 characters of that message are stored with a note and your name. In both cases the message may be written by someone else. Do not use them on messages that hold private information.

## Retention

What the code does today, row by row. "Not deleted automatically" means nothing in the code removes the row; it stays until you use erase (below) or the owner deletes it.

| Data | Retention in code |
| :--- | :--- |
| Account (`users`), builds, characters, sign-ups, memberships | Not deleted automatically. Removed by the erase route when it is switched on |
| Web sessions | 30 days, at most 10 per person. An expired session is removed when it is next presented or when the same kind of sign-in happens again, not on a timer |
| Saved settings (`user_state`) and their sessions | Not deleted automatically (sessions as above). `DELETE /me` removes them |
| Questions and answers (Ask) with their asker hash (`ask_turn_askers`), sheet edits, intent log, fact fixes | Not deleted automatically. The intent log is cleared when the owner's bot acknowledges it |
| Guest IP hash (`guest_rate_limits`) | Rows older than 2 hours are deleted the next time any guest asks, so a row can outlast 2 hours when no guest asks |
| Extra answer text behind "More" and "Sources" (`answer_details`) | 30 days, deleted by the scheduled job (only when the answer buttons are switched on) |
| Helpful / Off votes (`answer_feedback`) | 90 days, deleted by the scheduled job (only when the answer buttons are switched on) |
| Discord replay guard (`discord_events`) | The Discord interaction id and a one-way hash of the request body, about 10 minutes, deleted as new interactions arrive. The body itself is not kept |
| Votes and daily AI counters (`kv_state`) | Not deleted automatically |
| Audit log (`audit_log`) | Not deleted automatically (12 months is proposed) |
| Cloudflare request logs and AI Gateway logs and cached answers | Set by Cloudflare, not by this service (unverified; counsel to note). AI Gateway may cache an answer for up to 6 hours |
| Backups (D1 Time Travel) | Cloudflare's window (unverified; see `docs/runbooks/backup-restore.md`); deleted data can persist in backups until they expire |
| Owner backup files | The owner can pull a full export (`tools/pull-export.mjs`) and keeps it outside Cloudflare until deleted; it holds everything in the database except login secrets |
| Guild data after the guild removes the bot | Not deleted automatically (30 days then purge is proposed) |

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (counsel to confirm which laws apply and the response time): access, correction, deletion, export, objection or restriction, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to your regulator.

**What works today.** `DELETE /me` (with a web session) removes your saved settings and sessions and nothing else. The full export and erase (`GET /api/me/export`, `POST /api/me/erase`) are built and tested but are **switched off** (`DATA_RIGHTS` is off) until counsel and the owner turn them on; until then they answer 404. Until they are on, ask the owner (see Contact) to run an export or an erase for you.

**What the full erase removes**, once on: your account row and everything that hangs off it (sign-in identities, web sessions, memberships, characters, builds, sign-ups); your saved settings and sessions; your Helpful / Off votes; your ballot in each board vote; your daily AI counters; your Ask questions and answers with their "More" / "Sources" text (found by the asker hash, and for older questions by name), and, when you use your member key, the rows filed under your handle or name (sheet edits, dispatches, the intent log, and fact fixes). It leaves one audit row that says an erase happened and when, with no link to you.

**What it does not remove:**
- Your card and name in the shared board documents (`party`, `signups`). An admin edits those by hand.
- Your entry in the owner's Discord roster (`discord_members`) stays after an erase, so the bot still treats you as a member until an admin edits the roster.
- Your display name, your question and the party's names and builds, which were sent to the AI vendor, and the AI Gateway request logs that may keep the full request. This service cannot erase those; neither can it erase Cloudflare's and Discord's own copies, or backups until they expire.
- Questions asked on Discord by someone who is **not on the roster** are stored under their Discord nickname (and the intent log under "guest"). A nickname is never matched to a person, so nothing finds those by key. Web questions from a visitor are stored as "Visitor" with no identifier. Neither can be exported or erased on request; the owner would have to find them by hand. The guest IP hash used for rate limits is a separate row and is not linked to any question.
- Rows filed only under a handle or name: character-sheet edits, dispatches, the intent log and fact fixes are found by your handle or roster name (member-key request only; a web session cannot be tied to a handle). Questions asked **before** the 2026-11-03 change have no asker hash and are also found only by name, so if another person used a nickname equal to your roster name, such an old row cannot be told apart and is erased or exported with yours. New questions are found by hash, never by name.
- A member key that another running copy of the Worker already checked can still be accepted for read requests for up to 15 minutes after an erase. Requests that change data check the database again and refuse an erased key at once.
