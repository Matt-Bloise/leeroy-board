## How to search (free, do these before choosing)
Record every result, including nothing found, in the table below. Search the exact name, then close spellings and sound-alikes ("Rally board", "Rallybord").

- **USPTO TSDR / Trademark Search** (United States): go to uspto.gov, "Trademark Search" (this replaced TESS). Search the word mark; filter Live. Look at goods and services classes 9 (software), 42 (SaaS), 41 (games/entertainment), 38 (online communication). TSDR shows the status of a found serial number.
- **EUIPO eSearch plus** (European Union): euipo.europa.eu, eSearch plus; search the trade mark name, status Registered or Filed, check the same classes.
- **WIPO Global Brand Database** (international, many national offices): branddb.wipo.int; search the brand name, and include sound-alike matching.
- Also look, informally: a general web search, app stores, GitHub, npm, the Discord app directory.
- Unregistered ("common law") use matters too. A clear register is not a clearance. A trademark attorney should clear the chosen name before any spend on branding (roadmap Q42, Q57).
