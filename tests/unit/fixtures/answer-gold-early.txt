**Blizzard has not published a Forever earning guide, so the reliable early gold comes from Classic's usual mix of quest rewards, vendor trash, gathering professions, and the Auction House [systems/earning-gold~gold.earn.ways].**

- Quest rewards · [systems/earning-gold~gold.earn.quests] pay a small amount of gold directly, plus items you can vendor or list.
- Gathered materials · [systems/earning-gold~gold.earn.gathering] skinning, herbalism, mining, fishing and cloth farming supply what crafters buy.
- Auction House and vendors · [systems/earning-gold~gold.earn.auction_house] list materials or crafted goods there, and sell grey items to vendors [systems/earning-gold~gold.earn.vendor].

Street Rat knows all about earning gold, mostly by telling his paladin to pick up copper until the floor is clean.
