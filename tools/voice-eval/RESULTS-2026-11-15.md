# Voice A/B results, 2026-11-15

This page records the method and numbers of the first run of this harness. Fictional prompts only; no friend text, no roster names. Individual evaluation, not a causal claim about the free production model.

## Method

- 38 frozen fictional prompts (30 core, 8 "4b"). Hash frozen in `prompts.sha256` and `rubric.sha256`.
- Arms were answered by Claude subagents (no `claude -p`, no API key, no Llama arm). Arm3 is the production prompt with its deleters, Haiku-class; 17 of its 38 answers were written by code with no model call (abstain lines from the fixture library). Arm2 is the restored research-mode prompt, Haiku-class. Arm1 is the same restored prompt, Sonnet-class.
- 97 model or code answers. 6 of 97 replies had a mistyped token nonce (5 Sonnet, 1 Haiku) and were repaired mechanically; originals were kept as `*.answer.txt.orig`. The `--tolerant` option added with this page reports such a case instead of failing.
- All arms normalised identically (`--normalise`: sources lines, confidence tags, markdown stripped).
- 360 blind tasks: 2 comparisons x 38 prompts x 2 orders x full, voice and facts variants, in 60 fresh-context batches of 6.
- One judge (Codex, a different model family from the answerers). No second independent family, no owner spot check.
- Harness at a030ce72 plus the offline runner (`--emit-prompts`, `--ingest-answers`).

## Judge reliability

Order-swap consistency was 84.4% over 180 pairs against an 85% gate. The tool's own rule marks the judge UNRELIABLE, so no PASS verdict can be issued. A-share was 49.5% (95% interval 43.6 to 55.3).

## Length and independence

Answer length after normalisation, in characters: arm1 mean 318 and median 266; arm2 mean 237 and median 177; arm3 mean 168 and median 186. Length is a confound for voice. There are 5 writer clusters per arm; the intervals are optimistic.

## What the numbers support

- Voice only: the restored prompt on Haiku beat the production prompt on Haiku 14 to 1 with 12 ties.
- Combined and facts-only: the production prompt led 18 to 5 and 15 to 6. Part of that is structural, since 17 of 38 production answers are short code-written abstain lines with no specifics to get wrong.
- Between the restored arms, Sonnet led on voice (18 to 5) and Haiku led on facts (17 to 5) and on the combined question (11 to 6, 9 inconsistent).

## Not answered

- Whether the free Llama or Workers AI model caused the regression (no Llama arm).
- Whether Claude through the API sounds different from a subagent.
- Whether the original voice is matched (no reference arm, no can-it-tell test, no owner spot check).
- The 8 "4b" prompts were not scored.
