Guild companion

Your Discord forms and threads, turned into your group's next steps.

A guild companion for groups that plan in Discord. It reads the sign-up forms and the threads your group already uses, and shows who is in, what is open and what comes next, on one page.

How it works

  1. Your group fills in a form or talks in a thread in Discord.
  2. The companion gathers the answers into cards.
  3. Everyone sees the same next steps, in their own time zone.
