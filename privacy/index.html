Privacy notice (draft)

Reviewers: the owner and the back-end architect. The table-level source for this notice is docs/data-inventory.md. The statements about what is stored were checked against the code on 2026-11-03.

The Leeroy Living Board is an unofficial companion for guilds playing WoW Forever. It is a fan project, not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment. It runs on Cloudflare. This notice says what we hold about you, why, who else sees it, and what you can do.

We do not store your Discord avatar, your email address, or any password. Nothing in the current code stores a Google, Battle.net, Twitch or GitHub identifier or an OAuth token; the database has tables ready for them (identities, provider_tokens) but no feature writes to them, and this notice will change before one does.

Cloudflare, which hosts the service, sees and logs request details such as your IP address, request time and browser type under its own terms (see Retention). The service does not keep a copy of them.

Parts of the board are public. The documents behind the wall (party, signups, timeline, tonight and a few more) are served to anyone who asks the API, with no sign-in: if your handle, character or sign-up appears there, anyone can read it.

Your member key and what your browser keeps

  • Your member key is stored raw (not hashed) in your browser's localStorage under the name board-key. The server keeps only a salted hash of it, but your browser keeps the key itself, so anyone who can open your browser profile, or any script that runs on this site, can read it.
  • The key reaches the board in a link ending in #k=<key>. The page saves it and removes it from the address bar. The part after # is not sent to the server, but it can sit in your browser history and in anything you copy or share before it is removed. Treat a link with #k= like a password.
  • The key is sent in an Authorization header only to the board's own service (api.leeroy.party, a preview copy of it, or a service on your own computer). It is not sent anywhere else.
  • The board also keeps other settings in localStorage under names that start board- (such as your name, where you were and what you have seen) and short-lived copies of its data files under names that start bd:. "Forget me on this browser" removes every name that starts board- or bd:, including board-key.

The device check (/probe)

The device check page reads what your browser reports and shows it to you first ("Nothing has been sent"). Only if you press Send does the board send it, to POST /probe, together with your member key in the Authorization header, so the report can be tied to your member. The report is 39 fields: a type tag (kind) and a version number (v), plus 37 readings:

  • Browser and platform: engine, platform, whether it runs inside Discord, whether it has a Discord user agent, whether it is framed, whether it is installed as an app.
  • Vibration and haptics: whether the vibration API exists and ran, whether the switch haptic works, the vibration route used, and the strength you said you felt.
  • Motion sensors: whether the orientation and motion APIs exist, whether motion needs permission, the permission result, and how many orientation and motion events arrived in 3 seconds.
  • Input: number of touch points, coarse pointer, no-hover, pointer events.
  • Screen: screen width and height, window width and height, pixel ratio (times 100), visual viewport support, and the four safe-area insets (top, right, bottom, left).
  • Preferences and display: reduced motion, data saver, HDR screen, wide-gamut screen, battery API, screen wake lock.

Each value is a yes/no or a small number. No name, no Discord id and no free text is in the report. At the time of writing the service acknowledges the report and does not store it (unverified: Cloudflare request logs may still show that the request was made).

Message content disclosure

The bot answers slash commands and other interactions sent to it over HTTP (POST /discord). It does not listen to your channel messages (unverified; counsel and the owner should confirm no Message Content intent is enabled in the Discord developer portal). What you type into a command or into the Ask box is stored and sent to an AI vendor, so do not put anything private in it.

Two message actions are the exception. If you use the "Ask Leeroy" message action on someone's message, the text of that message (up to the question length limit) is stored as the question, in the Ask log and the intent log, and sent to the AI vendor. If you use "Fix this fact", the first 200 characters of that message are stored with a note and your name. In both cases the message may be written by someone else. Do not use them on messages that hold private information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (counsel to confirm which laws apply and the response time): access, correction, deletion, export, objection or restriction, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to your regulator.

What works today. DELETE /me (with a web session) removes your saved settings and sessions and nothing else. The full export and erase (GET /api/me/export, POST /api/me/erase) are built and tested but are switched off (DATA_RIGHTS is off) until counsel and the owner turn them on; until then they answer 404. Until they are on, ask the owner (see Contact) to run an export or an erase for you.

What the full erase removes, once on: your account row and everything that hangs off it (sign-in identities, web sessions, memberships, characters, builds, sign-ups); your saved settings and sessions; your Helpful / Off votes; your ballot in each board vote (whether it is kept inside the vote document or, once migration 0010 is applied, as its own row in the per-ballot table; the export lists both, and a request is refused with a stated maximum, changing nothing, if the service ever holds more than 720 votes); your daily AI counters; your Ask questions and answers with their "More" / "Sources" text (new questions are found by the hashes above; only questions that already existed when migration 0009 was applied are found by name), and, when you use your member key, the rows filed under your handle or name (sheet edits, dispatches, the intent log, and fact fixes). It leaves one audit row that says an erase happened and when, with no link to you.

What it does not remove:

  • Your card and name in the shared board documents (party, signups). An admin edits those by hand.
  • Your entry in the owner's Discord roster (discord_members) stays after an erase, so the bot still treats you as a member until an admin edits the roster.
  • Your display name, your question and the party's names and builds, which were sent to the AI vendor, and the AI Gateway request logs that may keep the full request. This service cannot erase those; neither can it erase Cloudflare's and Discord's own copies, or backups until they expire.
  • Questions asked on Discord by someone who is not on the roster are stored under their Discord nickname. A new one also carries a hash of their Discord id, so a request made with that Discord sign-in (web sign-in, when it is switched on) finds it; such a person has no board key. A question from before migration 0009 carries no hash and nothing finds it by key; the owner would have to find it by hand. A nickname is never matched to a person. The copy of such a question in the intent log is filed under the handle "guest" and is not removed by any person's request; it is cleared when the owner's bot acknowledges the log.
  • Questions asked by a web visitor without a key are stored as "Visitor" with no identifier and cannot be exported or erased on request. The guest IP hash used for rate limits is a separate row and is not linked to any question.
  • Which credential finds which question: a member-key request finds the questions you asked with the key and the ones you asked on Discord as a roster member (both carry your handle hash). A web sign-in finds the ones you asked on Discord (they carry your Discord-id hash) but not the ones you asked with a key alone, which carry only your handle hash.
  • Rows filed only under a handle or name: character-sheet edits, dispatches, the intent log and fact fixes are found by your handle or roster name (member-key request only; a web session cannot be tied to a handle). Questions that already existed when migration 0009 was applied have no hash and are found only by name, so if another person used a nickname equal to your roster name, such an old row cannot be told apart and is erased or exported with yours. No question written after that is ever matched by name.
  • A member key that another running copy of the Worker already checked can still be accepted for read requests for up to 15 minutes after an erase. Requests that change data check the database again and refuse an erased key at once.

Contact

Email privacy@leeroy.party or ask a guild admin to pass your request on. Say that you want to use your data rights (access, export or erasure) and what the request is about; it is read by the service operator and answered privately. Do not put secrets in the message.

Children

The service is not for anyone under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live if higher. Discord requires users to be 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if we learn we have, we delete it. (Counsel to confirm the threshold and any parental-consent duty.)

International transfers, security, changes

Cloudflare and the AI vendors may process data outside your country; counsel to confirm the transfer mechanism. Member keys are stored only as salted hashes. Session tokens are stored only as one-way hashes of random 256-bit values (not salted, which is safe for random tokens). No system is perfectly secure. We will update this notice with a date when it changes.
