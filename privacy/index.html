Privacy notice (draft)

DRAFT. Every legal point here needs review by counsel before publication. Some facts are marked (unverified) or (proposed) and need owner decisions. This is not legal advice. Reviewers: the owner and the back-end architect. The table-level source for this notice is docs/data-inventory.md. The statements about what is stored were checked against the code on 2026-11-03.

The Leeroy Living Board is an unofficial companion for guilds playing WoW Forever. It is a fan project, not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment. It runs on Cloudflare. This notice says what we hold about you, why, who else sees it, and what you can do.

We do not store your Discord avatar, your email address, or any password. Nothing in the current code stores a Google, Battle.net, Twitch or GitHub identifier or an OAuth token; the database has tables ready for them (identities, provider_tokens) but no feature writes to them, and this notice will change before one does.

Cloudflare, which hosts the service, sees and logs request details such as your IP address, request time and browser type under its own terms (see Retention). The service does not keep a copy of them.

Parts of the board are public. The documents behind the wall (party, signups, timeline, tonight and a few more) are served to anyone who asks the API, with no sign-in: if your handle, character or sign-up appears there, anyone can read it.

Message content disclosure

The bot answers slash commands and other interactions sent to it over HTTP (POST /discord). It does not listen to your channel messages (unverified; counsel and the owner should confirm no Message Content intent is enabled in the Discord developer portal). What you type into a command or into the Ask box is stored and sent to an AI vendor, so do not put anything private in it.

Two message actions are the exception. If you use the "Ask Leeroy" message action on someone's message, the text of that message (up to the question length limit) is stored as the question, in the Ask log and the intent log, and sent to the AI vendor. If you use "Fix this fact", the first 200 characters of that message are stored with a note and your name. In both cases the message may be written by someone else. Do not use them on messages that hold private information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (counsel to confirm which laws apply and the response time): access, correction, deletion, export, objection or restriction, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to your regulator.

What works today. DELETE /me (with a web session) removes your saved settings and sessions and nothing else. The full export and erase (GET /api/me/export, POST /api/me/erase) are built and tested but are switched off (DATA_RIGHTS is off) until counsel and the owner turn them on; until then they answer 404. Until they are on, ask the owner (see Contact) to run an export or an erase for you.

What the full erase removes, once on: your account row and everything that hangs off it (sign-in identities, web sessions, memberships, characters, builds, sign-ups); your saved settings and sessions; your Helpful / Off votes; your ballot in each board vote; your daily AI counters; and, when you use your member key, the rows filed under your handle or name (sheet edits, dispatches, the intent log, your Ask questions and answers with the "More" / "Sources" text, and fact fixes). It leaves one audit row that says an erase happened and when, with no link to you.

What it does not remove: your card and name in the shared board documents (party, signups) and the owner's Discord roster, which an admin edits by hand; copies at Cloudflare (logs, AI Gateway cache), Discord and the AI vendor; and backups until they expire. A web-session erase cannot reach rows filed under a handle or name, because a Discord display name is chosen by its owner and could equal someone else's; use your member key for those. Guest questions are tied to a hashed IP address, not to you, and cannot be found by your id.

Children

The service is not for anyone under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live if higher. Discord requires users to be 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children; if we learn we have, we delete it. (Counsel to confirm the threshold and any parental-consent duty.)

International transfers, security, changes

Cloudflare and the AI vendors may process data outside your country; counsel to confirm the transfer mechanism. Member keys are stored only as salted hashes. Session tokens are stored only as one-way hashes of random 256-bit values (not salted, which is safe for random tokens). No system is perfectly secure. We will update this notice with a date when it changes.
